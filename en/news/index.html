News

What changes in REALMSHARD, taken from the development log.

Welcome to the official site

31 October 2026

REALMSHARD now has its own site, in French, English and German: the trailer, the three realms and their 39 classes, the game modes, an FAQ and this news page.

The Download page gathers the apps for Windows, macOS and Android, with honest instructions to get past the warnings of unsigned files. On iPhone and iPad, the game plays in Safari and can be added to the Home Screen.

The site sets no cookies, has no trackers and uses no external fonts.

Accounts, verified ranked and phone comfort

31 October 2026

Accounts: create one with an e-mail address and a password; a confirmed e-mail opens leaderboards, ranked and the War of the Realms. You can delete your account from the game, and gift codes are here.

Ranked: ranked games are verified by the server, which replays the same rules as your game.

On phones: bigger finger targets, text that stays readable, a Codex on three big columns with a “Discovered only” filter, the “Use full screen” option and very light “Haptics”.

In duels: bosses have their lines, a button speeds up the AI’s turns and a recap follows every game. New music also comes with the modes and realm ranks.

Björn Rankbreaker, the Infiltrator and the Squire’s Path

31 October 2026

The Svealand Raider becomes Björn Rankbreaker, a legendary Shadowblade card with new art.

New players follow the Squire’s Path: ten first steps, no time limit, each with its reward, and the “Squire of Camelot” title at the end of the path. After the champion pick, boosters of that class come with the first days.

Arena, Quick Expedition, Daily Challenge and realm ranks

30 October 2026

Arena of Emain Macha: pick a hero out of three, draft 30 cards and chase 12 wins before your third loss; from 4 wins on, two boosters await you.

Quick Expedition: one act of 8 floors with a guaranteed elite and the boss, in 15 to 20 minutes. Daily Challenge: the same champion, campaign and modifiers for everyone, one scored attempt a day and its leaderboard.

Realm ranks from rank 1 to rank 10, with each realm’s titles, weekly quests on top of the daily ones, and the season ranking. Act bosses drop a booster of your run’s class or realm.

2v2 team duels, act cinematics and three AI levels

30 October 2026

Team duel (2v2): two heroes per team play at the same time on one shared board. Each player commands their own minions, and online duels are refereed by the game’s host.

Act cinematics: every Story act opens with a narrated scene, and guides welcome each mode on your first visit.

Three AI levels, clearer cards, fixed spell damage, a new soundtrack and graphics settings gathered in one place.

REALMSHARD’s first steps

15 May 2026

Development starts in mid-May 2026: the pixel-art engine, a first fight between an Armsman and a bandit, then the classes of Midgard and Hibernia, relics, events and the four acts up to the World Spire.

Since then, the game has moved to card duels with mana from 1 to 10, 39 classes and more than 1,400 cards.
