The game in 3 points

  1. Fast-paced card duels

    Play minions, cast spells and break the enemy hero. Mana grows from 1 to 10 over the turns, with 1,400+ cards and 130+ legendaries to collect.

  2. A roguelike campaign per realm

    Pick your path on the map, face elites and dragons, earn relics and improve your deck. Every act opens with a narrated cinematic.

  3. Three realms at war

    Albion, Midgard and Hibernia: 13 classes each, every one with its hero power and cards. Defend your realm in duels, 2v2, ranked and the War of the Realms.

Game modes

  • Story

    Your realm’s campaign in 4 acts, then the Abyss: map, elites, bosses and relics. 60 to 90 minutes.

  • Quick Expedition

    One condensed act: 8 floors, a guaranteed elite and the boss. 15 to 20 minutes, great on a phone.

  • Daily Challenge

    The same challenge for everyone: set champion, campaign and modifiers, one scored attempt a day and its leaderboard.

  • Duel

    Face the AI (three levels) or other players online with your decks, casual or ranked, season after season.

  • Team duel (2v2)

    Two heroes per team, one shared board and simultaneous team turns, against the AI or with your friends.

  • Arena of Emain Macha

    Pick a hero out of three, draft 30 cards one by one and chase 12 wins before your third loss.

  • War of the Realms

    Pledge allegiance, earn Realm Points, rank up and help your realm take keeps and towers.
